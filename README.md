
# StyleSync

## Overview

StyleSync is a mobile-first fashion discovery and shopping platform designed to simplify online clothing shopping.

It combines visual search, personalized fashion recommendations, inspiration boards, universal wishlists, and shopping alerts into a single platform.

Users can discover clothing that matches their personal style, follow their favorite stores, and receive updates about new arrivals, restocks, and price changes from supported retailers.

StyleSync also aims to help fashion retailers reach interested customers through store accounts and product integrations.

## Problem Statement

Online fashion shoppers often switch between multiple websites to discover clothing, compare products, and check for new arrivals or restocks.

Finding clothes that match a specific aesthetic or a saved Pinterest inspiration image can be time-consuming. Even after finding a suitable product, shoppers may discover that their preferred size or colour is unavailable.

Existing shopping experiences often separate inspiration, product discovery, wishlist management, and availability tracking across different platforms.

StyleSync aims to bring these activities together into one convenient experience.


## Proposed Solution

StyleSync aims to provide a unified platform where users can discover, save, organize, and track clothing from multiple online stores.

Users will be able to upload outfit inspiration images and discover similar products using AI-powered visual search. They can organize their favorite items into personalized boards, follow stores, and receive notifications about new arrivals, restocks, and price changes.

Fashion retailers will also be able to create store accounts and manage product information through supported integrations, helping improve the accuracy of product availability updates.

The platform will provide the same core functionality across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop devices.

## Target Users

StyleSync is designed for people interested in fashion and online shopping.

### Primary Users
- Fashion enthusiasts who regularly explore online clothing stores.
- Shoppers who save outfit inspiration from Pinterest and other platforms.
- People who want to track new arrivals, prices, and product availability.
- Students and young adults who enjoy discovering and organizing fashion inspiration.

### Secondary Users
- Small and medium-sized fashion retailers.
- Online clothing stores looking to reach interested customers and maintain updated product catalogs.
